QA professional with an academic background in Systems Analysis, experience in Manual Testing and API Testing, and a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence quality.

My approach combines analytical thinking, attention to detail, structured evaluation criteria, and critical analysis to assess AI-generated content and identify inconsistencies, inaccuracies, and potential quality issues.

## Portfolio Overview

This repository contains demonstration projects focused on evaluating AI-generated responses through structured guidelines, scoring rubrics, annotated examples, and quality reports.

The examples are synthetic and designed for portfolio demonstration purposes.

## Evaluation Areas

### 1. Accuracy and Factual Consistency
- Identify factual inaccuracies.
- Detect unsupported statements.
- Recognize hallucinated information.
- Assess consistency and reliability.

### 2. Instruction Following
- Evaluate compliance with user instructions.
- Identify missing requirements.
- Assess format and constraint adherence.

### 3. Relevance and Completeness
- Determine whether responses address the user's intent.
- Identify irrelevant or incomplete information.
- Evaluate coverage of requested content.

### 4. Clarity and Communication
- Assess readability and organization.
- Identify ambiguous or confusing statements.
- Evaluate language quality and coherence.

### 5. Safety and Responsible AI
- Identify potentially harmful content.
- Evaluate appropriate handling of sensitive requests.
- Review responses for unsafe recommendations.

## Evaluation Methodology

The portfolio uses structured evaluation criteria and scoring guidelines to ensure consistent and explainable assessments.

Each sample evaluation includes:
- Input prompt.
- AI-generated response.
- Evaluation criteria.
- Assigned scores.
- Identified quality issues.
- Reviewer justification.
- Suggested improvements.
